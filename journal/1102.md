## Vision

### Why learn things that come hard to you? 

To never cease in persisting in working to do great things. The goal is not accomplishing great things, instead to work towards it. Happiness comes in working towards the goal, not accomplishing it. For what would life be if you were only happy after you accomplish an excelent goal? 
Where would your heart be at in the evenings? 
"This world gives you comfort, but you were not made for comfort. You were made for greatness!"- JPII
The world we live in gives noise. The internet is a beautiful thing God has let man create but to be selective of content you digest is a daily battle we will undergo every single day. 
YouTube's upload rate is 500 hours every minute. What that looks like is if you consumed 16 hours every day for 60 years, you would consume around 350,000 hours. You would waste 60 years of your life on roughly half of what is uploaded to YouTube in a single day. And that is just YouTube- billions more hours live in text.  
In total it is around 5-10 billion hours, with millions more being added every single year. 
We need to be selective and intentional in what we consume, not to let our mind be swayed in in what the algorithms delivers today but instead to find content that enables the soul to breathe. 
To be able to think, to build, that is the American Dream.
